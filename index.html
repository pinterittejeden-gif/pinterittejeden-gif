What plants sense, how they move material around, and whether any of it can be put to use.

BioLume Sentinel

Could a plant indicate which patches of soil are worth testing? The idea is simple until the soil itself is considered, because soil is a complex biological and mineral system. The write-up covers reporter systems and the practical problems that limit them.

Where it gets difficult The signal has to be specific to the target.

Metallophyte-Armor

Could a tree move copper into the tissue that needs protecting, so the wood lasts longer? I hit a mass-balance problem almost immediately. Getting a metal into a tree and getting it to the right place turned out to be two different problems.

Where it gets difficult Uptake is not the same as delivery to the right tissue.

Ghost-Graft

Could dodder be used as a delivery route between two plants? I would bridge them, move one labeled protein across, then remove the dodder. Dodder already moves material between hosts on its own. Controlling what it carries is the part I could not solve easily.

Where it gets difficult It pulls resources from the plants it connects and can pass viruses along.

Materials that grow, settle, or repair.

These sit between ecology and materials science, where the material itself affects the outcome.

Coral-Suture

Could a small mineral tile give coral larvae a better surface to settle on, then be collected later? The tile seems plausible to me. Whether the larger system around it is worth building is still open.

Where it gets difficult Settlement changes with species, site, and season.

Titan-Mycelium

A self-healing material is only useful if it can also be made to stop healing. I limited the scope to one sealed, replaceable unit and a single controlled pulse of growth. Whether the fungus can be controlled is a separate question.

Where it gets difficult Growth has to start when I want it to, and stop just as dependably.

Finding the pollution that arrived between samples.

I wanted a passive sampler that catches a short pollution event routine sampling would miss.

Water Receipt

Could a sealed cartridge record a pollution pulse that happened days before collection? Water passes in, the organisms stay inside, and a permanent DNA change leaves a record.

Where it gets difficult It has to stay responsive in real wastewater without slowly switching itself on.
